## About the Library
The U.S. Department of Labor Library, established in 1917, is one of the oldest Cabinet-level libraries.
U.S. Department of Labor Libraries Catalog
Search the Wirtz Labor Library and MSHA Technical Information Center & Library collections:
U.S. Department of Labor employees can access digital subscriptions to newspapers, journals, and other periodicals by using the library search page on LaborNet. The Wirtz Labor Library also has a large historical collection, only accessible through a card catalog search. [Contact the library](mailto:library@dol.gov) for assistance. ***Appointments are required for the public***.
