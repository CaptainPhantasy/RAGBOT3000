## Technical Assistance
WDQI grant recipients may be in various stages of workforce longitudinal database development. Some states are in the initial development stages while others are expanding upon fully implemented systems. ETA has contracted Social Policy Research (SPR) to provide technical assistance to grantees.
Please visit the [**WDQI Technical Assistance**](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/wdqi/technical-assistance) webpage for more information and resources.
