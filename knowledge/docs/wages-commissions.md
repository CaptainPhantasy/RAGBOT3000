A sales commission is a sum of money paid to an employee upon completion of a task, usually selling a certain amount of goods or services. Employers sometimes use sales commissions as incentives to increase worker productivity. A commission may be paid in addition to a salary or instead of a salary. The [Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)](/agencies/whd/flsa) does not require the payment of commissions.
[A fact sheet regarding commissions](/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/20-flsa-commissions-retail) is available from the [Wage and Hour Division's](/agencies/whd) website.
Read the [federal statute](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-779) on this topic.
