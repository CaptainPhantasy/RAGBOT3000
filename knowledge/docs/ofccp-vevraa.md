# Vietnam Era Veterans' Readjustment Assistance Act | U.S. Department of Labor

[The Vietnam Era Veterans' Readjustment Assistance Act](/agencies/ofccp/vevraa/as-amended) (VEVRAA) is a law that prohibits federal contractors and subcontractors from discriminating in employment against protected veterans and requires employers take affirmative action to recruit, hire, promote, and retain these individuals.
