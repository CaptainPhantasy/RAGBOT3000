The [Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA)](/agencies/ebsa) provides a [web page dedicated to consumer information on retirement plans](/agencies/ebsa/workers-and-families/additional-protections) to provide retirement plan participants and their beneficiaries with information on their rights under federal law with respect to their plans. The information ranges from general information on the law to numerous specific issues.
