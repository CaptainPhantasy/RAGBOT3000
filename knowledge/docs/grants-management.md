## [Managing Your ETA Competitive Grant](/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/grants/pdfs/ETA_Grantee_Handbook.pdf)
The Grantee Handbook is a general resource designed to orient grantees to the grant process in ETA. The Handbook is sent to the Authorized Representatives and Points of Contact identified in the grant application at the time the grant agreement is transmitted. Note that this is only a resource and specific details about your grant may differ. Please check your grant agreement and direct questions to your Federal Project Officer.
## [SMART Grant Management Training](https://dol.gov/agencies/eta/grants/SMARTtraining)
SMART is a DOL technical assistance initiative started by ETA’s Office of Grants Management (OGM) in 2015.  Since its inception, SMART has trained 21,000+ grant recipients, subrecipients, stakeholders, service providers, workforce partners, and Federal staff.
SMART is for everyone involved in the management of a grant and its life cycle.
