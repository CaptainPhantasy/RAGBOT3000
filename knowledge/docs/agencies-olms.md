# OLMS | U.S. Department of Labor

## Online Public Disclosure Records
![blue lined paper icon](/sites/dolgov/files/OLMS/homepage/img/icons/lined_paper_icon2.jpg)
### [File Labor-Management Reports](/agencies/olms/reports/electronic-filing)
Go to the OLMS Electronic Forms System (EFS) to file your LM report.
![blue megaphone icon](/sites/dolgov/files/OLMS/homepage/img/icons/megaphone_icon2.jpg)
### [View Persuader and Information-Supplying/Employer and Consultant Reports](https://www.persuader-reports.gov/)
Find reports concerning employers hiring third parties to supply information to them concerning the employers' workers or persuade them concerning their union organizing rights. Search for Form LM-10 reports filed by employers and Form LM-20 and LM-21 reports filed by a labor relations consultant.
![blue papers icon](/sites/dolgov/files/OLMS/homepage/img/icons/papers_icon2.jpg)
### [View Union Reports](/agencies/olms/public-disclosure-room#union)
Find Form LM-1 Information Reports, Forms LM-2, LM-3, LM-4, and simplified Labor Organization Annual Financial Reports, and union constitutions and bylaws. Also, find Form LM-30 Labor Organization Officer and Employee Reports and Form LM-15/15A/16 trusteeship reports.
![blue people icon](/sites/dolgov/files/OLMS/homepage/img/icons/people_icon2.jpg)
### [View Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBA)](/agencies/olms/regs/compliance/cba#cba)
Find CBAs received voluntarily from either an employer or a labor union (or their employees or agents), singly or jointly.
