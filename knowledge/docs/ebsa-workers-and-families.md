# Workers and Families
Know your rights. Know your choices.
## Resources to help navigate life’s events
Life can change in an instant. EBSA has resources to guide you through your health and retirement benefit options during life’s major moments so you know exactly what to do.
After using the resources below, learn more about [additional protections](/agencies/ebsa/workers-and-families/additional-protections) that may apply to your health and retirement benefit plans during your employment.
