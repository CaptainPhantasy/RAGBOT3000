# Opinion Letters
Requests for interpretations and other rulings under Title I of ERISA are managed by the [Office of Regulations and Interpretations](/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/about-us/organization-chart#ori), following guidelines established by [ERISA Procedure 76-1](/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/resource-center/advisory-opinions/filing-requests-for-erisa-aos). This office responds to inquiries from individuals and organizations by issuing advisory opinions or information letters. **Advisory opinions** apply the law to specific facts, while **information letters** highlight well-established principles or interpretations.
