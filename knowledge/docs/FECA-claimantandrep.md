## Federal Employees Program
***Subscribe to Email Updates***
*The Federal Employees Program periodically provides claimants and representatives with information about the FECA program on topics to include new procedures, eligibility for benefits, and available services. To receive these updates by email, please* [*sign up for email alerts here*](https://public.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDOL/subscriber/new?topic_id=USDOL_598)*.*
### General Information on Claims filed under the Federal Employees' Compensation Act (FECA)
[Basic Information on New Claims](/agencies/owcp/FECA/regs/compliance/Basic-Information-on-New-Claims) - Basic information on the initial handling of new claims filed under the FECA.
[Federal Employees’ Compensation FAQs](/agencies/owcp/FECA/fec-faq) - Answers to Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about the FECA.
[Communication Assistance and Accommodations](/agencies/owcp/FECA/regs/compliance/assistanceandaccommodations) - Information on assistance available for injured workers who require special accommodations to communicate with OWCP.
[Find a Medical Provider](https://owcpmed.dol.gov) - A resource for locating a medical provider for treatment of a work-related injury or illness.
[Employees' Compensation Operations & Management Portal (ECOMP)](https://www.ecomp.dol.gov/) - Injured workers should use ECOMP to electronically upload documents to existing case files. Injured workers employed by certain agencies should also use ECOMP to electronically initiate claims for injury or illness on Forms CA-1 and CA-2, claims for wage loss on Form CA-7, and reports of injury or illness on Form OSHA-301.
