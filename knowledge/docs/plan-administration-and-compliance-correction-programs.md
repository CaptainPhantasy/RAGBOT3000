# Correction Programs
The Employee Benefits Security Administration's correction programs make it easy for you to comply with the law and fix violations. The **Voluntary Fiduciary Correction Program (VFCP)** is a way for plan officials to correct violations and avoid excise taxes for prohibited transactions. The **Delinquent Filer Voluntary Compliance (DFVC) Program** gives plan administrators a way to avoid higher civil penalty assessments by satisfying the program's requirements and voluntarily paying a reduced penalty.
