# Reasonable Accommodation Pocket Card | U.S. Department of Labor

The "Requesting a Reasonable Accommodation" pocket card assists applicants, employees and other interested parties in understanding the process for requesting a reasonable accommodation. Printed copies can be obtained by calling the Customer Service Help Desk at 1-800-397-6251.
