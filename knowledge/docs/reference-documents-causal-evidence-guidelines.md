## Highlights
* CLEAR reviews causal studies in a number of labor-related topic areas and assigns them a rating indicating the strength of their causal evidence.
* This document provides the CLEAR evidence criteria for rating causal studies. A companion addendum provides additional examples of how to apply the guidelines to rate the strength of causal evidence.
* CLEAR assesses the quality of evidence for:
  + randomized controlled trials (RCTs)
  + interrupted time series (ITS) designs
  + comparison group designs
  + other nonexperimental causal designs (e.g., difference-in-differences)
