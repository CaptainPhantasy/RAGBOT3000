# Non-Binding PY 2023 WIOA MSG Performance Assessment Results | U.S. Department of Labor

The [workbook at this link](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/eta.opdr.dp/viz/Non-BindingMSGPerformanceResults/Non-BindingMSGPerformanceResults?publish=yes) reflects the non-binding outcomes of the Departments’ (Department of Education and Department of Labor) assessment of state performance on the Measurable Skills Gain (MSG) primary indicator of performance. The Departments of Labor and Education issued a notice indicating which primary indicators of performance will be assessed for PY 2023. The notice was published as [Training and Employment Notice (TEN) 04-24](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/ten-04-24), [Program Memorandum 24-7](https://www2.ed.gov/policy/adulted/guid/memoranda.html), and [FAQ 24-02](https://rsa.ed.gov/sites/default/files/subregulatory/FAQ-24-02.pdf) for each agency. In that notice, the Departments committed to publishing the non-binding results for the indicators that were not formally assessed.
For PY 2023, the performance indicators being formally assessed are:
For the WIOA title I and IV Programs (WIOA Adult, WIOA Dislocated Worker, WIOA Youth, Vocational Rehabilitation):
* Employment Rate 2nd Quarter after Exit
* Median Earnings 2nd Quarter after Exit
* Employment Rate 4th Quarter after Exit
* Credential Attainment Rate
For the WIOA title III program (Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service):
* Employment Rate 2nd Quarter after Exit
* Median Earnings 2nd Quarter after Exit
* Employment Rate 4th Quarter after Exit
* Overall Program Score
Note: The Departments determined that the available baseline data were not sufficient to produce reliable estimates using the required statistical adjustment model for all performance indicators for the WIOA title II Adult Education and Family Literacy Act (AEFLA) program; therefore, AEFLA results were not included in this analysis.
