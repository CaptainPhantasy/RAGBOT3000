# Training and Employment Guidance Letter No. 04-21
**Issue Date:**
December 14, 2021
**Program Year:**
2021
**Fiscal Year:**
2022
**Expiration Date:**
Continuing
Subject
Modification Requirements for Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) State Plans for Program Years (PYs) 2022 and 2023
Purpose
This Training and Employment Guidance Letter (TEGL) conveys to states the Administration's priorities, modification requirements, submission process, and deadline for the required modification of the WIOA Unified and Combined State Plans (State Plans) for PY 2022 and PY 2023, consistent with Sections 102 and 103 of WIOA.
