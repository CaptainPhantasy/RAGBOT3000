* [Federal minimum wage](/general/topic/wages/minimumwage)

* [Child labor requirements of the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)](/agencies/whd/child-labor)
## Government Contractors
The Wage and Hour Division also enforces labor requirements of the following:
* [Davis-Bacon and Related Acts](/agencies/whd/government-contracts/construction),
* [McNamara O'Hara Service Contract Act (SCA)](/agencies/whd/government-contracts/service-contracts),
* [Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards Act](/agencies/whd/government-contracts/cwhssa), and
* [Copeland Anti-Kickback Act](/agencies/whd/government-contracts/copeland-anti-kickback).
Discussion of these acts appears in the [Government Contracts](/general/topic/wages/govtcontracts) subtopic under the [Wages](/general/topic/wages) topic. The Wage and Hour Division has a variety of compliance materials available for both employees and employers on the subject of work hours.
