## Accessibility Statement
The Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs (OFCCP) at the U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) is committed to providing access to all individuals who are seeking information from our FOIA Reading Room and to comply with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended. Section 508 requires that all individuals with disabilities (whether they are federal government employees or members of the general public) have access to, and use of information and data, comparable to that provided to individuals without disabilities, unless an undue burden would be imposed on OFCCP.
Any individual with a disability who has difficulty accessing documents and materials related to the FOIA Reading Room should contact the National Office OFCCP FOIA staff at 202-693-0101 or [OFCCP\_NO\_FOIA@dol.gov](mailto:OFCCP_NO_FOIA@dol.gov).
This request should include:
1. nature of the accessibility problem encountered,
2. URL (web address) of the page or pages in question,
3. specific information sought, and
4. contact email address or phone number for a response.
OFCCP staff will respond promptly and work to provide the information being sought.
