# About VETS | U.S. Department of Labor

## Our Vision
To enable all veterans, service members, and military spouses to reach their full potential in the workplace.
## Our Mission
We **prepare** America's veterans, service members, and military spouses for meaningful careers, **provide** them with employment resources and expertise, **protect** their employment rights, and **promote** their employment opportunities.
## Our Values
Integrity, Commitment, Respect, Excellence and Innovation.
## Our Priorities
1. Getting the military to civilian employment transition right.
2. Leveraging partnerships to maximize employment outcomes.
