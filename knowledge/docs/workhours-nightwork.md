Extra pay for working night shifts is a matter of agreement between the employer and the employee (or the employee's representative). The [Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)](/agencies/whd/flsa) does not require extra pay for night work. However, the FLSA does require that covered, nonexempt workers be paid not less than time and one-half the employee's regular rate for time worked over 40 hours in a workweek.
