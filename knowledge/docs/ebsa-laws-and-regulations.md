# Laws and Regulations
Stay informed with the important rules governing job-based benefits.
## Key Laws
Browse [all key laws](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws).
* ### 
  ### [Affordable Care Act (ACA)](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/affordable-care-act)
  This act adds many protections related to employment-based group health plans.
  ![affordable care icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/affordable-care-act/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_ACA.png?itok=AtC7xqxn)
* ### 
  ### [Children’s Health Insurance Program Reauthorization Act (CHIPRA)](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/chipra)
  This act creates special enrollment opportunities for employees and dependents who are eligible for, but not enrolled in, a group health plan.
  ![chipra icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/chipra/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_CHIPRA.png?itok=7CznEoPT)
* ### 
  ### [Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act (COBRA)](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/cobra)
  This act provides a way for workers and their families to temporarily maintain their employer-provided health insurance during situations such as job loss or a reduction in hours worked.
  ![cobra icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/cobra/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_COBRA.png?itok=6w8BRITl)
* ### 
  ### [Employee Retirement Income Security Act (ERISA)](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/erisa)
  This act provides critical protections that have safeguarded workers retirement, health, and other job-based benefits.
  ![erisa icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/erisa/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_ERISA.png?itok=X8QelJyV)
* ### 
  ### [Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) of 2008](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/mental-health-and-substance-use-disorder-parity)
  This act  provides federal protections for certain types of health plans.
  ![mhpaea icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/mental-health-parity/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_MHPAEA%20of%202008.png?itok=X2d14702)
* ### 
  ### [No Surprises Act](/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/no-surprises-act)
  This act protects patients from unexpected medical bills when they receive certain types of out-of–network care.
  ![no surprises act icon](http://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/styles/large/public/EBSA/laws-and-regulations/laws/no-surprises-act/EBSA_New%20web%20pages_Icons_Grey%20box_Laws%20and%20Reg_Key%20Laws_No%20Surprises%20Act.png?itok=76YU7Wsc)
