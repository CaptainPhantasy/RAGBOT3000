The Employment and Training Administration (ETA) Performance website will assist you in understanding how the workforce system measures, reports, and evaluates performance under the [Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/wioa "Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act") (WIOA). This site includes content on primary focus areas and resources to help grantees and stakeholders access information on WIOA results, reporting, and guidance.
ETA focus areas include:
* Overseeing the performance accountability provision of WIOA to obtain valid, accurate, and comparable results across states to inform policy and program decision making;
* Coordinating with the Department of Education on the establishment of WIOA indicators of performance;
* Managing the Workforce Data Quality Initiative (WDQI); and
* Overseeing the maintenance of wage record exchange systems for state and other grantees to verify performance outcomes.
