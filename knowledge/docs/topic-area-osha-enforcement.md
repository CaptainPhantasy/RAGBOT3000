* # OSHA Enforcement
  Established by the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970, [OSHA’s mission](http://www.osha.gov/about.html) is “**to assure safe and healthful working conditions** for working men and women by setting and enforcing standards and by providing training, outreach, education and assistance.” This topic area focuses on research determining the effectiveness of different OSHA-led enforcement activities in reducing the occurrence of **workplace illnesses, injuries, exposure to hazards, and/or deaths**.
  **Status:** Literature reviewed in this topic area currently covers 1979 - 2012.

Findings: 
* According to the research, there is some evidence that OSHA inspections reduce injury rates, on average.
* Some recent research has strong current relevance and provides valuable descriptive information, but low causal evidence on the impact of inspections.
* There is little information on the characteristics of OSHA inspections and other OSHA activities.
