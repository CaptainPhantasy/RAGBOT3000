# Miscellaneous Cases | U.S. Department of Labor

## Overview
The OALJ Law Library has separate libraries for the more commonly adjudicated case areas, such as whistleblower, immigration-related labor issues, Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, and OFCCP. The library you are in now covers the wide variety of the remaining case areas.
## Employment and Training Administration
The Employment and Training Administration administers various federal government job training and worker dislocation programs, federal grants to states for public employment service programs, and unemployment insurance benefits programs. Some of these programs can involve disputes that result in a hearing before a DOL ALJ. ETA-related [immigration](/agencies/oalj/topics/libraries/LIBINA) and [WIA](/agencies/oalj/topics/libraries/LIBJTP) cases are set out in separate collections. Because Job Service Complaint System disputes are usually (but not always) immigration-related, they are found in the  [Immigration](/agencies/oalj/topics/libraries/LIBINA) collection. OALJ and ARB materials relating to other ETA-programs are found in this collection.

## Wage and Hour Division
The Wage and Hour Division (WHD) is responsible for administering and enforcing a wide variety of labor laws, such as the Federal minimum wage, overtime pay, recordkeeping, and child labor requirements of the Fair Labor Standards Act. WHD also enforces the Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act, the Employee Polygraph Protection Act, the Family and Medical Leave Act, wage garnishment provisions of the Consumer Credit Protection Act, and a number of employment standards and worker protections as provided in several immigration related statutes. Additionally, WHD administers and enforces the prevailing wage requirements of the Davis Bacon Act and the Service Contract Act and other statutes applicable to Federal contracts for construction and for the provision of goods and services. OALJ has separate libraries covering [DBA and SCA](/agencies/oalj/topics/libraries/LIBDBA) adjudications, and [immigration related](/agencies/oalj/topics/libraries/LIBINA) WHD enforcement actions. ## Other DOL Programs
This collection includes case types that are not agency specific or cases types about which only a small number of hearings are typically conducted. These include:
**Decisions:**
* [The Debt Collection Act](/agencies/oalj/PUBLIC/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS/REFERENCES/CASELISTS/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS_CASELIST#DCA) (DCA). These cases can arise from any agency within DOL.
* [Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act](/agencies/oalj/PUBLIC/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS/REFERENCES/CASELISTS/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS_CASELIST#PFCRA) (CRA)
* [Petitions for modifications of mine safety standards](/agencies/oalj/PUBLIC/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS/REFERENCES/CASELISTS/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS_CASELIST#MSA) (MSA). These cases arise out of the Mine Safety and Health Administration.
* [Miscellaneous](/agencies/oalj/PUBLIC/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS/REFERENCES/CASELISTS/OTHER_DOL_PROGRAMS_CASELIST#MIS) case types (MIS) (e.g., standing orders; attorney admission and discipline; orders disposing of matters because DOL does not have jurisdiction over the filing, etc.)
