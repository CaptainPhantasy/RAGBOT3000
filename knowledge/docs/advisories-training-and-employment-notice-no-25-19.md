# Training and Employment Notice No. 25-19
**Issue Date:**
June 8, 2020
**Program Year:**
2019
**Fiscal Year:**
2020
Subject
Understanding Postsecondary Credentials in the Public Workforce System
Purpose
The information in this notice is intended to assist the public workforce system in better understanding the key elements of credentials and identifying credentials that help individuals acquire and leverage the skills needed for quality in-demand jobs that meet the needs of the business community.
