Through the Employment and Training Administration, the U.S. Department of Labor funds employment services to assist employers with worker recruiting, hiring, training, and more. The [CareerOneStop Business Center](https://www.careeronestop.org/BusinessCenter/default.aspx?frd=true) provides online resources and toolkits, while the approximately [2,400 American Job Centers across the country provide local employment services and assistance](https://www.careeronestop.org/BusinessCenter/Toolkit/find-american-job-centers.aspx).
Employers can also visit [Employer.gov](https://www.employer.gov/) to find common questions about their role as an employer.
