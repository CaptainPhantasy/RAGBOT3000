## Requests on 2021 data
OFCCP is posting this information in response to several Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests the agency has received for the Type 2 Consolidated EEO-1 Report data (EEO-1 data) for all federal contractors for EEO-1 filing year 2021. OFCCP has received three or more requests for the same information and thus the agency will make any responsive information available for public inspection in its FOIA Reading Room if and when it becomes available, as required by FOIA. See 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2)(D)(ii)(II). At the time this request was made, OFCCP only had the EEO-1 data for reporting year 2021 in its possession, this information is limited to that dataset.
After receiving these FOIA requests, consistent with Executive Order 12600 and Department of Labor FOIA regulations at 29 CFR 70.26, OFCCP notified federal contractors of the requests through several different methods, including (1) publication in the Federal Register; (2) postings on OFCCP’s website; (3) distribution to OFCCP’s GovDelivery listserv; (4) individualized e-mails to federal contractors; and (5) individualized letters sent through U.S. Mail to federal contractors. In these notices, OFCCP provided the opportunity for contractors to object to the disclosure of this information and informed contractors that if no objection to disclosure was received, the requested EEO-1 data would be disclosed.

## Requests on 2016 – 2020 EEO-1 data
OFCCP is posting this information in response to several Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests the agency has received for the Type 2 Consolidated EEO-1 Report data (EEO-1 data) for all federal contractors for EEO-1 filing years 2016-2020. OFCCP has received three or more requests for the same information and thus is making this information available for public inspection in its FOIA Reading Room as required by FOIA. See 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2)(D)(ii)(II).
After receiving these FOIA requests, consistent with Executive Order 12600 and Department of Labor FOIA regulations at 29 CFR 70.26, OFCCP notified federal contractors of the requests numerous times through several different methods, including (1) publication in the Federal Register; (2) postings on OFCCP’s website; (3) distribution to OFCCP’s GovDelivery listserv; (4) individualized e-mails to federal contractors; and (5) individualized letters sent through U.S. Mail to federal contractors. In these notices, OFCCP provided the opportunity for contractors to object to the disclosure of this information and informed contractors that if no objection to disclosure was received, the requested EEO-1 data would be disclosed.
**The EEO-1 data posted here is limited to those federal contractors that either affirmatively agreed to, or did not object to, the release of their EEO-1 data in response to the multiple notifications OFCCP distributed.** Without an objection to release, OFCCP has no legal basis to withhold disclosure under FOIA.
For contractors that have submitted objections, OFCCP is in the process of considering those objections and will make determinations as to whether the information provided must be disclosed or may be withheld under FOIA Exemption 4. In the event that OFCCP determines that a contractor’s EEO-1 data must be disclosed, OFCCP will provide the objecting contractor with written notice of its determination and a specified disclosure date, which will be set to occur at a reasonable time after the determination notice is issued.
***Litigation Update:***The Department is currently in litigation regarding whether 2016-2020 EEO-1 data of objecting contractors must be released, or whether it can be withheld as commercial confidential information under FOIA. The case is [Center for Investigative Reporting, et al, v. U.S. Department of Labor, Case No 24-880 (9th Circuit)](https://www.justice.gov/oip/ctr-investigative-reporting-v-us-dept-labor-no-22-07182-2023-wl-8879244-nd-cal-dec-22-2023). The Ninth Circuit heard oral arguments in this matter on February 14, 2025. A decision is pending.
