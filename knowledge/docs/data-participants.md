The TAA program collects and reports information on program participation as well as the benefits and services provided to TAA participants. The Department does not release individual level data for TAA participants pursuant to laws governing the release of such information; however, annual and quarterly state-level aggregates of major measures are available.
For annual data on TAA participants, see [Overview of TAA Data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/tradeact/data).
Each of the following files provides state-level aggregates of participant data for each quarter.
* [Data for the current quarter](/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/tradeact/pdfs/Web_Data_PIRL_Cur_Qtr.xlsx) (Most recent: quarter ending 9/30/2024)
* [Data for the period 10/1/2017 to current quarter](/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/tradeact/pdfs/Web_Data_PIRL.xlsx) (PIRL data)
* [Data for the period 1/1/2014 to 9/30/2017](/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/tradeact/pdfs/Web_Data_TAPR.xlsx) (prior Trade Act Participant Report (TAPR) report data)
