# Immigration Collection: Board of Alien Labor Certification Appeals and Office of Administrative Law Judges | U.S. Department of Labor

## Overview
The Office of Administrative Law Judges (OALJ) adjudicates several types of immigration-related cases that originate from the Employment and Training Administration. The Board of Alien Labor Certification Appeals (BALCA) principally hears appeals brought by employers whose applications to certify non-U.S. citizens to work in the U.S. have been denied.
Individual ALJs also adjudicate cases involving non-immigrant worker programs, such as H-1B, H-2A and H-2B cases brought by the Wage and Hour Division. *See* [Information for Aliens and Employers](/agencies/oalj/topics/information/Information_for_Aliens).
Interested persons may obtain information about the status of a BALCA appeal or immigration case by using the [Case Status Lookup](https://www.oalj.dol.gov/OALJ_Case_Status.html).
