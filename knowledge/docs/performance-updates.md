## **Updated August 2025**
* The **WIOA Title I Local Board annual performance data for Program Year (PY) 2023** is linked [here](https://dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/results/states). The report includes local area level data from the three WIOA Title I programs (Adult, Dislocated Worker, and Youth), including counts of Participants and Participants receiving training,  percentages of participants involved with Registered Apprenticeship, and the WIOA Primary Indicators of Performance.
* **Upcoming changes to Reporting Requirements** are now available on the [ETA Reporting Page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/reporting "https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/reporting"). Implementation dates and the revised layouts to various performance reporting templates, including the Participant Individual Record Layout (PIRL) and Quarterly Performance Reports (QPRs), can be found on the page and will go into effect as early as PY 2025 Q1 report quarter (Quarter ending 9/30/2025) for some programs.
* Guidance related to **Joint DOL and ED requirements for Data Validation** ([TEGL 07-18, Change 1](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/tegl-07-18-change-1 "https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/tegl-07-18-change-1")) and the **Use of Supplemental Wage Information** ([TEGL 26-16, Change 1](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/tegl-26-16-change-1 "https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/advisories/tegl-26-16-change-1")) were published on July 10, 2025. Changes are focused primarily on incorporating the recently formalized Effectiveness in Serving Employer primary indicator of performance.
* **Workforce Integrated Performance System (WIPS) updates are available** on the [WIPS page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/wips) and [WIPS Newsletters & Release Notes page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/performance/wips/newsletter-release-notes).
  + Updates include Edit Checks, Workforce Development Boards (WDB) Reporting Codes, and more.
