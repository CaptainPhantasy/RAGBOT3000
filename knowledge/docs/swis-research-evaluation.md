The Department of Labor’s Employment and Training Administration (ETA) and its partners at the Department of Education, the Office of Career, Technical, and Adult Education (OCTAE), and the Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services, Rehabilitation Services Administration (OSERS/RSA) facilitate the review of research and evaluation proposals under the SWIS. For more information about research and evaluation under the State Wage Interchange System (SWIS) see Section IX.E of the SWIS Agreement.
