# FLAG Resources
As part of the Department's technology modernization initiative, the Foreign Labor Application Gateway (FLAG) system was developed to replace the legacy iCERT System, improve customer service, and modernize the administration of foreign labor certification programs. Below are links to resources for using your FLAG account and completing forms in the system.
