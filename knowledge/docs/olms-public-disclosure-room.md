# Online Public Disclosure Room | U.S. Department of Labor

From this page, you can search for and view reports filed by unions, union officers and employees, employers, labor relations consultants, and surety companies. For help navigating the Online Public Disclosure Room, please contact OLMS at 202-693-0123 or [olms-public@dol.gov](mailto:OLMS-Public@dol.gov).
Please also view the [OLMS Online Public Disclosure Room demo](https://youtu.be/mTNMtQLgnM0).
## View Reports and Constitutions and Bylaws Filed by Unions
* [Union Search](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/)  
  Find Form LM-1 Information Reports; Forms LM-2, LM-3, LM-4, and simplified Labor Organization Annual Financial Reports; and union constitutions and bylaws.
  + [Payer/Payee Search](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Union%20Reports/Payer/Payee%20Search/)  
    Find information about labor union payers and payees and generate user-defined reports.
  + [Officer/Employee Search](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Union%20Reports/Officer/Employee%20Search/)  
    Find information about labor union officers and employees and generate user-defined reports.
  + [Download Yearly Data](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/?&_ga=2.141380850.1500577326.1673271090-176283821.1673011123#Union%20Reports/Yearly%20Data%20Download/)  
    Download raw data in pipe delimited format of union financial information.
* [Labor Organization Officer and Employee Reports](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/?&_ga=2.222062808.1500577326.1673271090-176283821.1673011123#Other%20Reports/LM-30%20Union%20Officers%20and%20Employees/)  
  Find Form LM-30 reports detailing actual or potential conflicts of interest between the financial interests of union officers and employees and their unions.
* [Trusteeship Reports](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/?_ga=2.217999830.1500577326.1673271090-176283821.1673011123#Union%20Reports/Trusteeship%20Reports/)  
  Find Forms LM-15, 15A, and 16 reports concerning trusteeships imposed by parent unions on their subordinate unions.
* **Additional Information:** [Forms LM-1, LM-2, LM-3, and LM-4 Labor Organization Annual Reports](/agencies/olms/reports/forms/lm-1-lm-2-lm-3-lm-4), [Trusteeship Reports](/agencies/olms/compliance-assistance/trustee-requirements#rt), and [Form LM-30](/agencies/olms/compliance-assistance/labor-organization-officer-employee-reporting).
## View Reports Filed by Employers and Labor Relations Consultants, including "Persuader" Reports and "Information-Supplying"
* [Online Persuader Reports Search](https://oprs.dol.gov/)  
  To perform a quick search for reports filed by an employer or consultant name.
* [LM-10 Employer Reports](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Other%20Reports/LM-10%20Employer/)  
  Reports filed by employers concerning, among other areas, their hiring third-party labor relations consultants to supply them with information concerning their workers or persuade them concerning their union organizing rights.
* [LM-20 Agreement and Activities Reports](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Other%20Reports/LM-20/21%20Labor%20Relations%20Consultant/)  
  Reports filed by third-party labor relations consultants hired by employers to supply them with information concerning their workers or persuade them concerning their union organizing rights.
* [LM-21 Receipts and Disbursements Reports](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Other%20Reports/LM-20/21%20Labor%20Relations%20Consultant/)  
  Annual financial disclosure reports filed by labor relations consultants.
* [Advanced Search by Employer or Consultant Name](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/?&_ga=2.217999830.1500577326.1673271090-176283821.1673011123#Other%20Reports/Advanced%20Search/)
* **Additional Information:** [Forms LM-10, LM-20, and LM-21](/agencies/olms/compliance-assistance/employer-consultant-reporting)
## View Surety Company Reports
* [Online Surety Company Search](https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/#Surety%20Reports%20(S-1)/S-1%20Search/)  
  Find Form S-1 Surety Company Annual Reports filed by surety companies that issue any bond required by the Labor-Management Reporting and Disclosure Act (LMRDA) or the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (ERISA).
* **Additional Information:** [Form S-1 Information](/agencies/olms/compliance-assistance/bonding#sc) page
