## Workforce Integrated Performance System (WIPS) User Walkthrough Video Series
The WIPS Walkthrough video series consists of four short videos that are designed to familiarize new WIPS users to its interface, features, and functionality. At the end of the video series, you will be able to:
* Obtain a WIPS account;
* Sign into WIPS and navigate to key pages within the system;
* Upload your report files through WIPS;
* Check and correct errors in your report files; and
* Review and submit your reports for certification.
### Video 1: Getting Started in WIPS
In this first video, we will explain how to obtain a WIPS account and how to navigate the system once you have signed in.
### Video 2: Uploading Report Files to WIPS
In this video, you will learn the steps for uploading your report files to WIPS.
### Video 3: Checking & Correcting Report Errors
In this video, you will learn about the automated edit checks run by WIPS when a report file is uploaded. You will also learn how to check and correct errors found during the edit check process.
### Video 4: Reviewing & Certifying Reports
This video wraps up the walkthrough of WIPS by introducing the various reports available in WIPS and shows how to conduct a final review of your reports and submit them for certification.
Note: By selecting "CERTIFY," the grantee's authorized representative is indicating that, to the best of their knowledge, the data submitted is complete, true, and accurate.
