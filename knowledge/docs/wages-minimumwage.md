The federal minimum wage for covered nonexempt employees is $7.25 per hour.
Many [states](/agencies/whd/state) also have minimum wage laws. In cases where an employee is subject to both the state and federal minimum wage laws, the employee is entitled to the higher of the two minimum wages.
The federal minimum wage provisions are contained in the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). The FLSA does not provide wage payment or collection procedures for an employee's usual or promised wages or commissions in excess of those required by the FLSA. However, some [states](/agencies/whd/state) do have laws under which such claims (sometimes including fringe benefits) may be filed.
The Department of Labor's [Wage and Hour Division](/agencies/whd) administers and enforces the federal minimum wage law.
