# Contact OLMS | U.S. Department of Labor

OLMS promotes union democracy, protects union assets, advances labor-management transparency, protects employment rights of public transit employees, and highlights the important role unions play in the modern American economy. For more information, please visit [About OLMS](/agencies/olms/about).
