# Media Contacts | U.S. Department of Labor

Credentialed media may receive our national media releases via email. If you are a credentialed member of the media and would like to be added to a media distribution list, send your email address, title and telephone number; and the name and URL of your media outlet to [m-DOLPublicAffairs@dol.gov](mailto:m-DOLPublicAffairs@dol.gov).
**IMPORTANT: Do not submit media inquiries or other time-sensitive concerns to this address**.
*All other issues (i.e., questions or concerns from the general public, sales pitches, etc.) must be directed to the department's* [*National Contact Center*](/general/contact/contact-phone-call-center)*.*
