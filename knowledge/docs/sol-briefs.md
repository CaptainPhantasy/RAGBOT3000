The Office of the Solicitor files briefs in cases that the U.S. Department of Labor litigates as well as amicus ("friend of the court") briefs in cases of interest to the Department of Labor. We are providing significant briefs filed by the Office of the Solicitor and, in some cases, filed by the Solicitor General, to which the Department contributed. The briefs are organized by the Office of the Solicitor [division](/agencies/sol/divisions) that filed them.
