# New and Trending Research
CLEAR synthesizes trending research, allows users to search new releases by date of release, and conducts flash reviews of studies.
[Trending Research](/trending-research)
CLEAR uses a streamlined, rapid evidence review process to summarize existing and emerging evidence on trending research topics.
[New Reviews](/news_events)
CLEAR releases new summaries and synthesis reports throughout the year. Use the new reviews archive to find our past activities.
[Flash Reviews](/flash-reviews)
Flash reviews are either recently completed reviews in established topic areas or reviews of studies that have been nominated and fall within CLEAR’s eligibility criteria. The same review procedures and evidence guidelines are used for flash reviews as for all other studies reviewed by CLEAR.
[Systematic Annual Search and Review](/sasr-reviews)
The systematic annual search and review (SASR) aims to ensure CLEAR includes the most up-to-date literature on topics of interest to CLEAR audiences, including topics with larger and smaller amounts of evidence. To accomplish this, the SASR focuses on identifying labor-related research based on the time period it was released, rather than its topical focus.
