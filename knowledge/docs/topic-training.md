## [Employment and Training Administration](/agencies/eta)
* The U.S. Department of Labor's Employment and Training Administration funds job training programs to improve the employment prospects of [adults](https://www.doleta.gov/programs/), [youth](/agencies/eta/youth), and [dislocated workers](/agencies/eta/layoffs/workers).
* Programs are aimed at boosting workers' employability and earnings and are delivered primarily by states through the [American Job Center network](https://www.careeronestop.org/) and tailored to local economies.
* Find training opportunities online [CareerOneStop](https://www.careeronestop.org/).
  + [mySkills myFuture website](https://www.myskillsmyfuture.org/) identifies local training opportunities which can help bridge your skills gap as you build a bridge from your last job to your next job.
  + Find information on local training programs by contacting your local [American Job Center](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/find-american-job-centers.aspx) or contacting Employment and Training Administration's toll-free help line at 1-877-US2-JOBS.
## [Occupational Safety and Health Administration](https://www.osha.gov/)
* The Occupational Safety and Health Administration provides training programs aimed at improving [safety and health](/general/topic/training/safety) in the general workplace setting.
## [Mine Safety and Health Administration](https://www.msha.gov/)
* The Mine Safety and Health Administration provides [training programs](https://www.msha.gov/training-education) aimed at improving safety and health in the mining industry.
