Other benefits include a host of incentives provided by agreement between an employer and an employee (or the employee's representative).
Individual states, however, may require employers to provide with some of these benefits. It is important for employers and employees to check with their [state's Department of Labor](/general/location) to check appropriate requirements.
Most of these benefits are not required by law. In addition to the subtopics listed on this page, you may also want to know more about [Unemployment Insurance](/general/topic/unemployment-insurance) or [Workers' Compensation](/general/topic/workcomp), covered under other topics.
