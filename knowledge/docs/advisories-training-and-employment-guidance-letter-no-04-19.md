Subject
Waiver Authority for the INA Program and Implementation of Additional Indicators of Performance
Purpose
This guidance describes additional indicators of performance for the INA program, and provides guidance on submitting waivers to statutory and regulatory requirements, including to the primary indicators of performance at Section 116(b)(2)(A) of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA).
