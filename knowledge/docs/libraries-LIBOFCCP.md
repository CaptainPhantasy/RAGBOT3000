## **E.O. 11246 (revoked)**
**Executive Order**
[E.O. 11246](https://www.dol.gov/ofccp/regs/statutes/eo11246.htm) , as amended (prohibited discrimination in employment by federal contractors on the basis of race, color, religion, or national origin and required affirmative action to ensure equal opportunity in all aspects of their employment). On January 21, 2025, the White House and President Donald Trump issued an Executive Order, "[Ending Illegal Discrimination and Restoring Merit-Based Opportunity](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/ending-illegal-discrimination-and-restoring-merit-based-opportunity/)," revoking Executive Order 11246.
