# Complaint Process | U.S. Department of Labor

## SUBMITTING A PRE-COMPLAINT INQUIRY OR COMPLAINT
### Pre-Complaint Inquiry
When you submit a pre-complaint inquiry, OFCCP can help you determine if we are the right federal agency to handle the matter. OFCCP will not notify the employer you submitted a pre-complaint inquiry. **Submitting a pre-complaint inquiry form to OFCCP does not constitute filing a complaint.**
Click this link to access the [Pre-Complaint Inquiry Form](/sites/dolgov/files/OFCCP/Forms/OFCCP-Pre-Complaint-Inquiry-Form.pdf)
The pre-complaint inquiry form can be submitted electronically via email to: [OFCCPComplaintForms@dol.gov](mailto:OFCCPComplaintForms@dol.gov).
You may also fax the form to (972) 850-2651 or mail the form to:
U.S. Department of Labor  
Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs  
200 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Room C-3325  
Washington D.C., 20210
### Complaint
You may also choose to bypass the pre-complaint inquiry and instead file a complaint of employment discrimination with OFCCP. If you file a complaint of employment discrimination, OFCCP will notify the employer of the complaint.
Click this link to access the [Complaint Form](/sites/dolgov/files/OFCCP/Forms/OFCCP-Complaint-Form.pdf)
The complaint form can be submitted electronically via email to: [OFCCPComplaintForms@dol.gov](mailto:OFCCPComplaintForms@dol.gov).
You may also fax the form to (972) 850-2651 or mail the form to:
U.S. Department of Labor  
Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs  
200 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Room C-3325  
Washington D.C., 20210
### IMPORTANT DEADLINES
Submitting a pre-complaint inquiry form to OFCCP does not extend the period for filing a complaint.
Complaints must be filed with OFCCP within **300 calendar days** of the alleged action(s) taken by the employer.
**If you have 60 calendar days or fewer in which to file a timely complaint, bypass the pre-complaint inquiry process and file a complaint.**
