Extra pay for working during weekends is generally a matter of agreement between the employer and the employee (or the employee's representative). The [Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)](/agencies/whd/flsa) does not require extra pay for weekend work. However, covered, non-exempt employees must be paid at least one and one-half times their regular rates of pay for the time worked over 40 hours in a workweek.
