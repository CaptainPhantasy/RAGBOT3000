## Petition Filing Instructions
TAA petitions must be properly completed and filed with the appropriate state and federal offices.
* [TAA Petition Form](/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/tradeact/pdfs/petition.pdf)
**Petition Fax / Mailing Addresses:**
U.S. Department of Labor,  
Office of Trade Adjustment Assistance  
200 Constitution Ave NW  
Room N-5428  
Washington, DC 20210  
Phone: 202-693-3560 or 1-888-DOL-OTAA (1-888-365-6822)  
Fax: 202-693-3584, 3585  
Email: [taa.petition@dol.gov](mailto:taa.petition@dol.gov)
For information on how to fill out the form, please see the [Frequently Asked Questions page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/tradeact/petitioners/faq). If you have further questions, feel free to reach out to us by phone or email.
### Is your petition pending?
Want to know what benefits and services are available to you while your petition is pending? Please visit the [Career OneStop State Resource Finder](https://www.careeronestop.org/Toolkit/StateAndLocal/find-state-resources.aspx), [mySkills myFuture](https://www.myskillsmyfuture.org), or [My Next Move](https://www.mynextmove.org) pages.
### Are You Currently Covered by a TAA Petition?
Want to know what benefits are available to you? Learn about benefits and services by petition number, or visit your local [American Job Center](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/find-american-job-centers.aspx) to find out how to apply for individual benefits and services if your petition is certified.
### Benefits
If you would like to learn more about benefits in the TAA Program, visit our [Benefits and Services page](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/tradeact/benefits).
### Denied?
Go to [Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/tradeact/petitioners/faq#denied) to learn about your appeal rights.
