# Statistics | U.S. Department of Labor

The U.S. Department of Labor collects and publishes statistics on many topics related to the workforce and the economy. Much of our data comes from the department's [Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov).
Browse these categories or explore and download data through our [Open Data Portal](https://dataportal.dol.gov). We also have an [interactive search tool for enforcement data](https://enforcedata.dol.gov/) from our Employee Benefits Security Administration, Mine Safety and Health Administration, Occupational Safety and Health Administration and Wage and Hour Division.
Can't find what you're looking for? Call our toll-free hotline at 1-866-487-2365.
