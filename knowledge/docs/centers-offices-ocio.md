## About Us
The Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO) serves as the IT hub of the U.S. Department of Labor. We develop, maintain and protect IT solutions and data across our 27 agencies to enable mission outcomes through technology and service. OCIO continually enhances federal IT and digital capability with a focus on cybersecurity and customer experience to serve America’s wage earners, job seekers and retirees.
We also have a vision for our workforce, providing opportunities for staff to grow their skills and bring their innovative ideas to life. Our staff is comprised of some of the government’s best IT talent, and OCIO helps foster their expertise to develop a collaborative environment.
Looking for a way to advance your skills and learn from the best while also contributing to mission work that makes an impact? Look no further – OCIO is the team for you!
