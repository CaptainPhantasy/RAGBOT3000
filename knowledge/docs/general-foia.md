# Freedom of Information Act | U.S. Department of Labor

This page will help you request records from the Department of Labor.
Under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) anyone can request access to federal agency records. Like all federal agencies, we must disclose records requested in writing, but we may withhold records in full or in part under one of FOIA's [exemptions and exclusions](/general/foia/exemptions).

## Submit a request
Fastest option: Create an account in our [Public Access Link](https://dol.secureocp.com).
You may submit your request to [foiarequests@dol.gov](mailto:foiarequests@dol.gov) or by mail, fax or courier. However, doing so may delay our response.
[Find contact information for our agency components here](/general/foia/contacts).
Or submit your request to the Office of the Solicitor using the contact information below.
**Email**: [foiarequests@dol.gov](mailto:foiarequests@dol.gov)  
**Fax**: 202-693-5389, Attn: FOIA Staff
